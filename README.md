## Greetings! 👋

My name is Alexa, and I develop computational models for biomedical data.

### About Me

I have a Ph.D. in Biostatistics, and my research focused on building deep learning models for integrating biological data, including multi-omics, clinical imaging, and molecular profiles. My favorite projects are those where the ultimate goal is to improve human health, and I deeply value a patient-centric perspective in my work.

### My Favorite Programming Languages (ranked):

1. **Python:** my all-around champion, perfect for my machine learning and deep learning projects.
2. **R:** a close second, my go-to for statistical modeling and computational biology analyses.
3. **MATLAB:** an honorable mention for signal processing work.
